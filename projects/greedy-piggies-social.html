The Project

Greedy Piggies was developed as part of a university project focused on building and promoting a digital brand through social media.

Social Strategy

Content was planned around the target audience, platform behaviour and suitable posting schedules.

Results

The campaign increased the Instagram audience by 72 followers during the project period while providing practical experience analysing content performance and audience response.
