The Project

Elavew required a short homepage promotional video that could quickly demonstrate how the platform works for both candidates and employers.

The Approach

I created a structured walkthrough focusing on the platform's most important features while keeping the pace suitable for a homepage audience.

Screen recordings were combined with transitions, on-screen highlights, narration and music to help guide the viewer through the experience.

My Role

I handled the editing, timing, voice-over and music integration, creating the finished promotional piece from the recorded website material.
